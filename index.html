Turns the picture into a chunky 3D voxel model, like Minecraft or MagicaVoxel. Best with pixel art, sprites and icons on a plain or transparent background.

Brighter pixels become higher ground, and the picture's colors paint the land. Use a grayscale heightmap or a top-down map.

Uses your regular Claude chat. No API key and nothing extra to pay. Claude builds a low-poly model from simple shapes, which works for characters, monsters, weapons, buildings and whole map scenes.

  1. Press Copy prompt.
  2. Open Claude at claude.ai, paste the prompt, and attach your picture.
  3. Copy Claude's reply, paste it below, and press Build.

To change a model later, press Copy parts JSON, paste it into Claude with your change ("add a shield"), and paste the reply back here.

Export

.glb loads in three.js, Unity, Godot and Blender. Avatars keep their skeleton and animations. .json saves the settings so you can rebuild or edit the model later.
